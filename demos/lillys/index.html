Our Story

Serving Rhodes Since 2006

For almost two decades, Lilly’s has been a gathering place for locals, families, and friends.

By day, we serve fresh coffee and relaxed meals by the water.

By night, Lilly’s transforms into an Italian-inspired dining destination where good food, wine, and conversation come together.

From breakfast to dinner, we welcome guests seven days a week.
