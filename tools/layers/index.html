Tools / Image to Layers

image to LAYERS

Turn a flat poster, ad or social post back into layers: the background, each shape or picture, and the text as real, editable text. Drag the SVG into Figma.

Everything runs in your browser. Your images are never uploaded.

Options

Layers

    Works best on flat designs: solid or gently graded backgrounds, clear type. The font is matched to Archivo at the measured size, weight and spacing; swap in the real typeface in Figma. Hidden layers are left out of the download.
