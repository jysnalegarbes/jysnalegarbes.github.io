u2netp.onnx
-----------
U²-Net (small variant, "u2netp") by Xuebin Qin et al.
https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net
Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0:
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

ONNX export as distributed by rembg (MIT), https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg
(release v0.0.0, md5 8e83ca70e441ab06c318d82300c84806). Unmodified.

Used by the Background Remover at /tools/background/ ("Fast" model).

here; it is loaded from https://huggingface.co/studioludens/birefnet-lite-512.
